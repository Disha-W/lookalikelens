# Asset sources

Theme "detective noir" (approved Nov 20, 2026).

All visual assets in app/static/ are generated by the project itself
(Python/PIL scripts, fixed random seeds), inspired by reference moodboard
pins the owner supplied. No third-party photos or illustrations are used,
so no attribution or license obligations apply.

- street-bg.jpg: illustrated night street (Victorian gables, lit windows,
  gas lamp, cobblestones, fog) - page background.
- parchment-sheet.jpg: parchment paper texture - board and sheet surfaces.
- parchment-note.jpg: smaller parchment texture - pinned case notes.

Earlier preview iterations used two CC-BY Flickr photos and one CC0
rawpixel photo; none of those remain in the shipped theme.
